Hotkeys

Click a field and press the key you want. Backspace clears it, Esc cancels. Keys are matched by position, so they also work with a non-Latin input method on.
A warning is shown if a key clashes with Udemy's own keys (Space, ←/→, Shift+←/→, ↑/↓, F, M, B, ?).
